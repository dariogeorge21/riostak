# `app/contact/page.tsx`

Contact page entrypoint (Server Component).

Displays a dedicated, Dribbble & Awwwards-inspired institutional contact and engagement portal for RIOSTAK:
- **Hero Header**: Status badge with live-pulsing amber indicator (`Academic Secretariat Desk Active // Response Window < 48H`), monumental Space Grotesk headline, Newsreader serif thesis statement, and 4-metric quick operational indicators bar.
- **Split Transmission Console & Global Hubs**:
  - Left column (7 cols): Integrates client leaf `ContactForm` for topic-selected academic dossier transmission.
  - Right column (5 cols): Research Hub directory (`OFFICE 1`, `OFFICE 2`, `OFFICE 3`) with addresses, emails, phone numbers, and hub codes, accompanied by official review protocol cards.
- **Editorial Clarifications Grid**: 3-card FAQ covering Visiting Chairs & Fellowships, Curricular Licensing, and Pre-publication Monograph Embargoes.
- **Edge-to-Edge Baseline Ticker**: Full-width baseline bar with office stamps (`OFFICE 1`, `OFFICE 2`, `OFFICE 3`) and 2026 status mark.
- **Layout Shell**: Wrapped by the global `Header` and `Footer` in `app/layout.tsx`.
